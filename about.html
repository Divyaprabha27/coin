The Story of RetroVerse

Where pixelated memories meet the future of entertainment.

Born from the 80s

RetroVerse started as a garage collection of three renovated Pac-Man cabinets. Founded in 2023, our mission was simple: bring back the authentic social experience of the arcade era.

We spent years sourcing original boards, calibrating CRTs, and curating a collection that spans four decades of gaming history. But we didn't stop there. We added a modern twist with VR lounges and craft cocktails to elevate the experience for the adult gamer.

Our Core Values


Community

Gaming is better together. We build spaces where strangers become high-score rivals and friends.


Nostalgia

Preserving the art of the arcade. Every button press and joystick click should feel just like you remember.


Innovation

Respecting the past while embracing the future. We integrate cutting-edge VR alongside the classics.
